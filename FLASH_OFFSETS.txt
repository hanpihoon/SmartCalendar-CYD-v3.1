Smart Calendar Pro v2 - ESP32 flash layout

esptool.spacehuhn.com -> Chip: ESP32

0x1000   bootloader.bin
0x8000   partitions.bin
0xE000   boot_app0.bin   (if generated/required by your Arduino core build)
0x10000  firmware.bin

For the first flash, erase flash first if you are replacing unrelated firmware.
After Smart Calendar Pro is installed once, future firmware.bin files can be uploaded from the device's local web Setup page (OTA).
